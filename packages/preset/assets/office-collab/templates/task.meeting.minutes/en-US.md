# Meeting Minutes

## Meeting Metadata

Record meeting title, date/time, facilitator, participants, and linked context or agenda items.

## Key Decisions

Summarize agreed conclusions, rationale, and any formal Decisions to propose or link in the ledger.

## Action Items

List each concrete action item with owner, due date, expected artifact or outcome, and linked Task ID when created.

## Open Questions

Record unresolved questions, who owns investigating each item, and the target resolution date.
