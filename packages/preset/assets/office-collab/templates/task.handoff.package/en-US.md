# Handoff Package

## Owner Transfer

Record outgoing owner (`from`), incoming owner (`to`), effective timestamp (`effectiveAt`), and the scope of transferred responsibility.

## Deliverables

List every handed-off artifact under `artifacts/`, its concise summary, and its verified SHA-256 digest matching `artifacts/handoff.json`.

## Open Items

List all open or blocked follow-ups with unique `id`, `summary`, assigned `owner`, and concrete `nextAction`.

## Acceptance Criteria

List the explicit takeover criteria and known risks/mitigations the incoming owner needs to confirm continuity.
