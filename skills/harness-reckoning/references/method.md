# Method and operating model

Online self-reflection tends to recycle the current context. Persisting every reflection creates an append-only rule store whose size eventually reduces compliance. The safer operating model is offline replay over observable outcomes, followed by a small evidence-backed proposal and a human decision.

The six built-in signal families are sentinel health, failed redispatch/rework, corrected facts, short-lived decisions, abnormal runtime termination, and explicitly retired or obsolete rule text. Counts are candidate generators, not diagnoses. A corrected fact may demonstrate healthy epistemic discipline; a repeated dispatch may be legitimate iteration. The human reads the attached evidence before acting.

The report has an owner and a deletion condition: if it is not consumed, the schedule should be disarmed rather than spawning another reminder pipeline. Human adjudication terminates the recursion; the reckoner does not evaluate or rewrite its own judging criteria.
